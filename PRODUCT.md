# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are tech recruiters / hiring managers and founders or consulting clients evaluating AI engineering capability. They arrive with limited time and decide whether Solano is credible for production AI work and whether to contact him.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and writing surface for Solano de Zuasnabar (AI Engineer). It must communicate that he ships AI systems to production, present real experience and skills clearly, and feel high-quality in UI/UX so the craft of the site reinforces the claim. Secondary goals: contact (email / LinkedIn / GitHub) and reading blog posts.

## Positioning

Evidence-first AI engineering portfolio: production ownership, client delivery, and agent/tooling practice — not a generic “AI enthusiast” landing page. Quality of the interface is part of the proof.

## Operating Context

Visited on the web (desktop and mobile). Typical path: scan identity and role → experience → how he works with AI / skills → writing → contact. Blog articles are Markdown sources under `content/articles/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js App Router portfolio site.
- Routes: home, experience, blog listing, blog articles from `.md` files.
- **Sacred content (do not rewrite without explicit ask):** experience company/role/project copy; skills / knowledge text tied to companies and hard skills.
- **Allowed to change:** “How I work with AI” section (structure, presentation, and non-company-bound copy); visual system, layout, accents, blog chrome, nav/footer presentation.
- Undecided: long-term content volume for blog; whether consulting CTA should be stronger than email-only contact.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Solano de Zuasnabar / handle `solanodz`
- Role label: AI Engineer
- Location: Tucumán — Argentina
- Voice: direct, technical, production-oriented English copy already on the site
- Visual direction preference (user-stated, not a full design system yet): simple, minimalist, tasteful, modern; single accent palette rather than mixed greens/yellows/indigos

## Evidence on Hand

- Real experience entries (Pi Consulting, Tecnología BI, Develative) with project detail
- Agent skills / tool practice section
- Blog Markdown articles in `content/articles/`
- CV PDF and company logos under `public/`
- Do not invent clients, metrics, testimonials, or press

## Product Principles

1. Production credibility first — the site should make “ships AI to prod” obvious in seconds.
2. Protect factual work history and skills; redesign the frame, not the evidence.
3. UI quality is part of the argument — quiet, precise, modern restraint over decoration.
4. One accent system; neutrals carry hierarchy.
5. Fast scan paths for recruiters and founders; depth available for those who dig into experience and posts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific legal standard stated. Aim for clear contrast, keyboard-usable nav/links, and readable article measure as a baseline quality bar.
